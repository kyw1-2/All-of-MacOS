# Workspace and the main window in 3.4

## Workspace

Workspace opens with AOM's status, then what needs attention, then your tools.

- **Tools** shows Automation, Window Tiling, Capture, Clipboard, Audio and System Tools. **Productivity** shows
  Folder Rules, Text Actions, Files and Focus. Each tile shows the module's live count and one control at the
  bottom right: a switch, a button or an arrow. Click anywhere else on a tile to open its page.
- **Quick actions** run the same commands as their shortcuts: Capture Area, Capture Window, Record Screen, Copy
  Text, Clipboard History, Measure Screen, Drop Shelf, Focus Session, Mute Microphone and Window Switcher.
  Automations you pin in Automation appear after them.
- **Recent activity**, **Today** and **This Mac** sit at the bottom.

### Customize Workspace

Click the sliders button in the toolbar, or choose **View › Customize Workspace…**. You can:

- hide or show Tools, Productivity, Quick actions, Recent activity, Today and This Mac;
- hide or show each module tile and each quick action;
- move quick actions up or down;
- choose **Reset Layout** to go back to the first layout.

The status line and Needs attention always show, because they tell you when something is wrong.

On Workspace itself:

- To move a quick action, drag it onto another quick action. It goes in front of that one. You can also
  Control-click it and choose **Move Left** or **Move Right**.
- To hide a tile or quick action, Control-click it and choose **Hide from Workspace** or **Remove from Quick
  Actions**. A message with **Undo** appears for a few seconds, and **Edit › Undo** (⌘Z) also works.

## Sidebar

- The sidebar has four groups: Workspace, Tools, Productivity and Activity. Click a group's arrow (it shows when
  the pointer is over the group name) to fold it. AOM remembers which groups are folded.
- Control-click a page to open it, to turn its module on or off (for example Drag to Snap or Clipboard
  History), or to show or hide its tile on Workspace.
- Small badges show only when something isn't normal: ⚠ with a number when something needs fixing, **Paused**,
  **Rec**, **Off**, and **On** while a Focus session runs.

## Moving between pages

- The **‹ ›** buttons beside the page title go back and forward through the pages you visited. **View › Back**
  (⌘[) and **View › Forward** (⌘]) do the same.
- **View** lists every page. ⌘1 to ⌘9 open the first nine.
- AOM reopens on the page you last used.

## Menu bar menu

The menu bar menu keeps its sections: Capture, Clipboard, Windows and, when modules add items, Productivity.
**Keep Awake** and **Pause AOM** (in More) now keep one name and show a checkmark while they're on. Hold the
pointer over Keep Awake to see when it ends.
